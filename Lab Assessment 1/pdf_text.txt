OVERVIEW 
This lab activity is a part of the course assessment series that aims to determine a standard feature-based
tracking technique that enables the creation of augmented reality (AR) web-based applications. It uses 
computer vision technology to recognize and track the images in real-time and overlays the 3D object 
(as simple as 3D cube). This project brings AR feature-based marker tracking technique to the Web. 
Feature-based tracking is commonly used to build AR application while Web is the most ubiquitous 
computing platform. There are already billions of devices connected to the web that have access to a 
plethora of visual information. OpenCV is a library for general computer vision application development, 
with hundreds of algorithms and efficient implementation. With HTML5 and new client-side technologies 
today browsers are capable of handling more complex tasks. You are required to develop a web 
application to produce AR marker using a feature-based tracking technique. A captured image is 
converted into a grayscale image, and the grayscale image has created the features.  
[Image] [Grayscale] [Features] 
Then your application is able to position and orient virtual objects , such as 3D cube  on that generated 
features. Once your web-camera captures the image, the features are recognized and 3D object will appear on 
the top of it.
